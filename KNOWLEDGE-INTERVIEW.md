# Assistant knowledge — interview

Answer whatever you want under each question. **Skip anything you'd rather not
publish** — every answer here becomes readable by anyone who asks the assistant
the right question, so treat this file as public even though it isn't yet.

Short answers are fine. Fragments are fine. I'll turn this into
`src/lib/knowledge.ts`, which gets loaded into the assistant's system prompt.

**Rules the assistant will follow:**

- It answers only from what's in this file plus `content.ts`. If something isn't
  here, it says it doesn't know and points at your email — it does not guess.
- Blank answers are not gaps to be filled in creatively. They're silence.
- It never invents dates, numbers, salaries, employers or grades.

---

## 1 · Availability and logistics

1. Are you currently looking for work? Actively, or casually open?
2. When could you start?
3. Full-time, part-time, contract, internship — which are you open to?
4. Remote, hybrid, or on-site? If hybrid/on-site, how far can you reasonably commute from Consolacion?
5. Would you relocate? Within the Philippines? Abroad?
6. Are you open to night shift / US or EU hours?
7. Do you have reliable home internet and a working setup for remote work? Worth being concrete.
8. Any current commitments (studies, board exam, existing job) that limit your availability?

## 2 · Role and money

9. What job titles are you actually targeting? (e.g. junior fullstack, frontend, backend, IT support, QA)
10. What kind of company do you want — startup, agency, BPO, enterprise, freelance?
11. What's your salary expectation, in PHP per month? A range is fine.
12. Would you say that number publicly, or should the assistant deflect to "ask Hans directly"?
13. Are you open to unpaid or low-paid work for the right learning opportunity?
14. Are you taking freelance work right now? What kinds, and what do you charge?

## 3 · Background and the story

15. Why the move from IT support toward software? What actually triggered it?
16. How did you start programming, and roughly when?
17. Are you self-taught, course-taught, or school-taught? Which courses or resources mattered?
18. What was your capstone experience like — your specific role if it was a team?
19. Was the capstone a group project? If so, how many people and what did *you* own?
20. Anything about your OJT at Green's Compugadgets worth telling?
21. Any honours, awards, Latin honours, or notable grades you want mentioned?
22. Any certifications beyond the TESDA and Cisco ones already listed?
23. Do you speak/write English comfortably? Any other languages? (Bisaya, Tagalog — worth stating for remote roles.)

## 4 · Technical depth

24. Which three technologies are you genuinely strongest in right now?
25. Which on your skills list are you weakest in / still learning? (Honesty here makes the assistant trustworthy.)
26. Have you worked with Git in a team — branches, PRs, reviews? Or mostly solo?
27. Have you deployed anything to production before these two Vercel apps?
28. Have you written tests? Which kind, if any?
29. Any experience with Linux, servers, networking beyond the TESDA training?
30. Databases — how comfortable are you actually? Joins, migrations, indexes?
31. What's the hardest technical problem you've solved? Walk through it.
32. What's something you tried that didn't work, and what you learned?

## 6 · Working style

41. How do you prefer to work — lots of direction, or figure it out yourself?
42. Solo or team? Any real team experience to point to?
43. How do you handle being stuck? Be specific, it's a common interview question.
44. What do you want to be doing in two years?
45. What kind of work would you turn down?

## 7 · Personality and tone

46. How should the assistant sound when it talks about you — formal, plain, warm, funny?
47. Anything you'd like it to mention if the conversation is going well? (a hobby, an interest, something human)
48. Anything you absolutely do **not** want discussed? (family, health, religion, politics, relationship status, exact address, birthday, previous salary…)
49. If someone asks something personal that isn't covered here, should it deflect politely or just say "I only cover Hans's professional background"?

## 8 · Contact

50. If someone wants to hire you, what's the single action you want the assistant to push? (email, LinkedIn, resume download, phone)
51. Is your phone number OK to give out to anyone who asks, or email-only?
52. Should the assistant offer to hand over the resume PDF proactively, or only when asked?
